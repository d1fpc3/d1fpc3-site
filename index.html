Questions

The honest answers
Why are you selling a course if you already make money trading?+

I spent two years learning these concepts. If I put them out for free, nobody would find them valuable; nothing free gets studied. The price is what makes people take the work seriously.

What model will you teach me?+

I don't teach a mechanical model. I teach discretionary concepts that help you see NQ clearly. I don't believe price can be reduced to rigid rules; context and judgment matter, and discretionary interpretation is the better way to read the market.

What exactly do I get?+

A course on reading NQ's context and intention, our session breakdowns, the private member room, and the Echelon software itself — the app where all of it lives, on the web or the Windows desktop. The D1 GEX board sits in there too, for its subscribers. The detailed concepts stay inside Echelon.

Is this for beginners?+

No. You should already understand futures mechanics and have screen time on NQ, or be actively getting it.

Is this signals?+

No. Nobody calls trades. Echelon teaches you how to interpret the market for yourself. The room is for study and discussion, not a signal feed.

Refunds?+

No refunds. The product is information; once read, it can't be returned. One payment, access for good, updates included — the numbers live on the pricing page.

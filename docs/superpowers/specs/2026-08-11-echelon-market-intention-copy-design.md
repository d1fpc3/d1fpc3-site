# Echelon Market-Intention Copy Redesign

## Objective

Reposition the restored `d1fpc3.com` landing page around understanding NQ's intention. Echelon should no longer be presented as D1's written trading plan, a rigid set of rules, or a collection of named setups. It should be presented as a body of concepts that changes how a trader reads NQ.

The existing visual layout, pricing, purchase behavior, Discord integration, sign-in route, risk disclosure, and responsive behavior remain unchanged.

## Core Promise

Echelon teaches traders how to read the meaning behind NQ's movement: context, intention, confirmation, and practical application. The strongest transformation claim is that members will no longer view the market as a series of isolated candles.

The page must not promise prediction, guaranteed results, or advance knowledge of every move. “Understanding intention” means interpreting observable price behavior through the concepts taught in Echelon.

## Header and Navigation

The site identity in the header becomes:

> Echelon by D1

The navigation label “The plan” becomes “The concepts.” The access and member sign-in links remain.

## Hero

The headline becomes:

> Understand what NQ  
> is trying to do.

The supporting paragraph becomes:

> Echelon teaches the concepts behind price movement—how to read context, recognize intention, and understand why NQ is moving where it is. Once you see the market this way, you cannot unsee it.

The price and access facts remain: $200 once, lifetime access, no subscription, and the live Discord member count when available.

## Concepts Section

Rename the section from “The plan” to “The concepts.” Retain the note that the complete specifics stay inside Echelon.

Replace the four current entries with:

1. **Context** — Understand the conditions that give a move meaning instead of judging candles in isolation.
2. **Intention** — Recognize what price is seeking, what it is reacting to, and when its behavior changes.
3. **Confirmation** — Separate meaningful price behavior from noise before committing to an idea.
4. **Application** — Study the concepts through D1's session breakdowns and the private member room.

These entries communicate the progression of interpretation without publishing the paid curriculum.

## Audience Section

Keep the existing “Who it's for” structure but rewrite it around the new positioning:

- The visitor already trades NQ or understands futures fundamentals.
- The visitor wants to understand price rather than collect setups.
- The visitor is willing to study context instead of chase calls or signals.

The closing filter becomes:

> If you only want entries, Echelon is not for you.

## D1 Engine

Describe the D1 Engine as a chart indicator built around Echelon's concepts. It remains separate, in development, $25 per month when released, and unnecessary for using Echelon.

## Access

Keep the $200 one-time price, lifetime access, included updates, members area, private Discord, sign-in link, and no-refund policy. Replace any remaining “written plan” language with “Echelon concepts” or “course,” depending on context.

## FAQ

Update answers so they consistently describe:

- A course teaching concepts for reading NQ's context and intention
- D1's session breakdowns and private member room
- An intermediate audience that already understands futures mechanics
- Education and independent interpretation, not signals
- The D1 Engine as a separate optional visualization tool based on Echelon concepts
- Immediate access after purchase and the existing refund policy

The curriculum remains private because the detailed concepts are the paid product.

## Metadata

Update the document title, meta description, Open Graph description, and Product structured-data description so search and social previews use the same market-intention positioning.

Use this description consistently where length permits:

> Echelon teaches the concepts behind NQ price movement—how to read context, recognize intention, and understand what the market is communicating.

## Technical and Failure Behavior

No architecture or data-flow changes are required. The existing static HTML and inline CSS remain. Checkout-disabled behavior, live Discord member count, permanent Discord link, and members sign-in must continue to work exactly as they do now.

- Visual redesign or layout changes
- Changes to the Echelon member or admin applications

- Performance, profit, or prediction claims

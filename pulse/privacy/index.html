Pulse

Privacy Policy · Last updated 26 July 2026

Pulse is a restaurant host stand operations board. It tracks scheduled restroom checks and kitchen updates on a single tablet inside a restaurant.

We collect nothing

Pulse does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no third-party services.

What is stored, and where

The app records the times that scheduled tasks were completed. Each record contains a task type, a scheduled time, a completion time and a lateness in minutes. Nothing identifies a person.

These records are written to private storage on the device itself and never leave it. They are not backed up to any server operated by us, because we do not operate one.

Network access

Pulse makes no network requests. Every file it needs is bundled inside the app and it functions completely offline. The Android build declares the INTERNET permission only because the app framework serves those bundled files from a local address inside the app sandbox. No data is sent anywhere.

Permissions

Pulse requests no runtime permissions. It does not access location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, or any other sensitive capability.

Deleting your data

Because all data is local, removing it is entirely under your control. Clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app, permanently erases every record. Nothing survives elsewhere.

Children

Pulse is a workplace tool intended for restaurant staff. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

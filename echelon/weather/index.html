Kalshi temperature ladders · paper

A market maker posts offers and waits. Across 115,503,669 contracts of Kalshi's own record, the people posting offers on these markets earned $226,586 while the people taking them paid Kalshi $758,638 in fees. That money is not spread evenly: it sits at both ends of the price range and reverses in the middle. This desk watches whether the bot is standing in the right place, and whether it is first in line when it does. Nothing here places an order.

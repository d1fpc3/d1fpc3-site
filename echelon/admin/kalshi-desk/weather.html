The map

every maker in these markets, measured from settled records

Each print has one taker and one maker at one price, and the box settles at zero or one, so what makers earned is arithmetic on the exchange's own record. No model, no fill assumption. Makers are paid at both ends and taxed in the middle: sell the cheap boxes, buy the near certain ones, quote nothing between 20c and 80c. Every policy below is judged against this shape.

Are we first in line?

live, against the touch

The pool is a ceiling, not a forecast. Every cent of it went to a maker already in the queue when the taker arrived. A quote resting behind the touch is filled only once everyone ahead of it is, which is the one thing that broke the first backtest and the reason the newest policy beats the touch by a cent or stays out.

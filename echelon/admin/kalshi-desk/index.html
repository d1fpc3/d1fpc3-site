The edge

What the bot trades, and the evidence for it. One row per round, priced at the touch, net of the real taker fee, over the last 31 days. Recomputed nightly from the tape.

The market prices these at about 90 cents. They win about 94% of the time. Break-even after the fee is 90.5 cents, so the gap is the edge. It exists because the book prices more movement than a fifteen-minute round actually delivers, which leaves the side that is already ahead too cheap.

What we take

  • Book BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP or DOGE. The other five run 2 to 5 cent spreads that eat the whole edge.
  • Clock Nine minutes before the round closes.
  • Distance Spot at least 15 basis points clear of the strike, on the side we are buying. A favourite sitting on its strike is a coin flip wearing a favourite's price.
  • Price The leading side quoted between 85 and 95 cents. Bought at the ask, held to settlement.
  • A plus Off. Narrowing to this cell raises the edge to 3.8 cents but drops to 30 setups a day, which earns about half as much. It is also the one cut chosen by searching, so its significance is the softest number here.

Both cuts carry signal independently, and the edge is flat zero only where the tape is quiet and the book is pricing no more movement than it delivered. The effect is indifferent to which way the tape moved, which is what rules out a slow book and a plain taste for favourites, and leaves a volatility risk premium as the explanation.

Proof, per book

Recomputed from the tape every night, not pasted in. The error bar clusters on the expiry instant, because rounds that settle together lose together: five simultaneous positions are worth about 1.7 independent bets, so an unclustered figure overstates by roughly 1.7 times.

The settlement basis

Kalshi settles on the CF Benchmarks index averaged over the final sixty seconds. The desk records Coinbase, which runs below it. Each round's strike is the previous round's settlement value, so the gap is re-estimated from the last seven days and added to the distance before the gate reads it.

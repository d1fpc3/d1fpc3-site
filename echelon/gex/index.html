When it updates

One trading day, in order.

Times are New York. The levels come from open interest, which changes overnight, so the morning book is the one that matters most.

  1. 8:00 AMThe morning book

    Today's open interest lands and every level is rebuilt from it.

  2. Before the openOn TradingView

    The pre-open levels are published to the script.

  3. 9:30 AMOn your chart

    Levels show for the session, with the time of the print beside them.

  4. 10:30 AM and 2:45 PMRepublished

    The midday and late books, as 0DTE gamma shifts through the day.

  5. 4:00 PMOff after hours

    Hidden until the next session. If a print ever runs late, the chart says the levels are old.

Questions

The straight answers.

How do I get it on my chart?

At checkout you enter your TradingView username. The invite-only script is added to your account automatically, usually within 15 minutes. On TradingView open Indicators, then Invite-only scripts, and add D1 GEX.

Which charts does it work on?

NQ and MNQ, with levels from QQQ options, and ES and MES, from SPX options. The Book setting picks the right one from your chart's symbol. Any timeframe.

When do the levels update?

They are rebuilt from each morning's open interest and published to TradingView before the open, around 10:30 AM and around 2:45 PM New York time. The chart always says when your levels are from, and levels from an earlier day draw faded and dashed.

Why are the levels gone after the close?

They show from 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM New York time on weekdays, on every timeframe. The setting Hide when market closed keeps them on if you want them overnight.

Do I need an Echelon membership?

No. D1 GEX is sold on its own. It also comes with every Echelon membership, in the app under Indicators, with the live board.

Is this financial advice?

No. Dealer positioning is modelled from open interest, never observed. Treat every level as a zone that needs price to confirm it, not a line that trades itself.
